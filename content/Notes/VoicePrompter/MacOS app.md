#voiceprompter #mac 
## Who It's For

Anyone on a Mac who records, presents, or takes calls \- and wants their notes right in front of them without anyone else seeing.

Especially useful for: webinar hosts, sales reps, course creators, demo recorders, online coaches, anyone on regular video interviews, and anyone presenting in a second language.

## Use Cases

* Recording YouTube videos, tutorials, or educational content  
* Delivering sales pitches or product demos on Zoom or Teams  
* Hosting webinars without losing your place  
* Presenting on video calls without losing eye contact  
* Appearing on interviews — job, podcast, or press  
* Recording online courses and training material  
* Presenting in a second language with confidence  
* Recording podcast episodes, intros, and scripted segments

## Features

### Voice & Script Sync

1. Voice-Activated Scrolling  
   * The script automatically scrolls in sync with the speaker's voice, pausing when they stop and resuming when they continue. Powered by a custom, best-in-class word-matching algorithm.  
2. Auto Language Detection  
   * Automatically identifies the language of pasted text and sets the correct recognition engine \- no manual selection needed.  
3. 60+ Language Support  
   * Supports over 60 languages and dialects via Apple's native speech recognition APIs.  
4. Bracket Handling  
   * Intelligently skips bracketed stage directions like \[smile\] or \[pause\] so they don't interfere with voice sync.  
5. Click-to-Sync  
   * Tap any word on screen to set the active reading position. When the speaker resumes, the app tracks the voice from exactly that word.

### Overlay & Window Behavior

1. Invisible Overlay Mode  
   * A floating, semi-transparent window sits on top of any other app \- so the speaker can read while looking directly at the camera.  
   * The overlay is invisible during screen sharing in Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, FaceTime and any other app \- visible only to the speaker, hidden from participants and recordings.  
   * Also invisible in screenshots and screen recordings.  
2. Always-on-Top  
   * The window persists over all desktops and full-screen applications. It never gets buried behind other windows.

### Script Management

1. Script History  
   * Stores up to 100 recent scripts in a scrollable list for quick access.  
2. In-Window Editing  
   * Scripts can be edited directly inside the prompter window without closing the overlay.  
3. Paste from Clipboard  
   * One-click loading of new text from the clipboard directly into the prompter.  
4. Copy to Clipboard  
   * Any script from the Recent section can be copied back to the clipboard instantly.

### Display & Reading Settings

1. Font Size Adjustment  
   * Font size is adjustable from to suit different screen distances and reading preferences.  
2. Paragraph Spacing  
   * Paragraph spacing is adjustable to control visual breathing room between sections.

### System Integration

1. Menu Bar Access  
   * The app lives in the macOS menu bar for fast access at any time.  
2. Launch from Clipboard  
   * Scripts can be launched directly from clipboard content via the menu bar.  
3. macOS Services Integration  
   * Scripts can be sent to the prompter from any text editor via the macOS Services menu.

### Onboarding & UX

1. Unlimited Demo Script  
   * A pre-loaded welcome script is available for free practice indefinitely, with no subscription required.  
2. Tutorial Link  
   * A persistent Tutorial link in the menu bar (with a one-time notification dot on first launch) leads to a Notion-based guide.

### Privacy

1. Fully On-Device  
   * All speech recognition and text processing happen locally on the Mac. Nothing is sent to any server.  
2. Local Script Storage  
   * Scripts are stored privately on the device and never synced or uploaded.
