# Project Overview

## Mission

I have two missions for both products

Web version:  
Give every creator a free voice-scrolling teleprompter that's reliable and effortless to use.

Mac app:  
Help anyone on a Mac nail their recordings and calls \- with an invisible floating teleprompter that follows their voice.

## Vision

A world where anyone can speak on camera with the same confidence as reading from a page.

## Product Ecosystem

VoicePrompter exists as two products that serve different audiences and work together as a funnel.

The [[Web app]] is free, open source, and works on any device in the browser. It is the top-of-funnel product \- the trust-builder. It attracts the broadest audience and introduces people to the VoicePrompter experience with zero friction and zero cost.

The [[MacOS app]] is the premium, native product sold via the Mac App Store. It is the monetization layer. Users in need of specific  features that are only possible by tight system integration like invisible screen-share overlay, always-on-top window \- are natural upgrade candidates.

## Growth strategy

### Primary engine: YouTube 

YouTube drives the majority of current traffic and remains the highest-leverage channel. The core approach is educational content targeting specific teleprompter topics people are already searching for \- essentially SEO on YouTube, where competition is lower and audience trust is higher than Google for this category.

Supporting channels (active or testing):

* Short-form video (Instagram, TikTok) \- driving awareness  
* Directory listings \- passive discoverability  
* X / Twitter \- community engagement and occasional commentary  
* Articles / written content \- supporting Google search presence  
* Influencer outreach \- testing partnership deals  
* Paid ads \- being tested as a supplementary acquisition channel

Content hypothesis: People searching for teleprompter help on YouTube are high-intent users. Educational content targeting specific use cases will rank in search and convert viewers into users organically.
