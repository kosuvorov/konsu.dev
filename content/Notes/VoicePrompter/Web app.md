#voiceprompter #web 

Last updated: 29.03.2026
## Who It's For

Solo creators and small teams \- YouTubers, TikTokers, Instagram Reels creators, podcasters, teachers, coaches, and public speakers \- who need a reliable, free, privacy-first teleprompter that works on any device without installation. Ideal for anyone who records themselves regularly and wants to eliminate memorization without paying for software or being locked into a specific platform or OS. Especially useful for people who own physical teleprompter hardware/glass.

## Use Cases

* Recording short-form video content for YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram  
* Filming tutorials, explainers, or educational content on a phone or tablet  
* Using a physical teleprompter glass rig with a phone or tablet as the display  
* Practice runs and script rehearsal on any device

## Features

### Voice & Script Sync

1. Voice-Activated Scrolling  
   * Real-time speech recognition automatically scrolls the script in sync with the speaker's voice. Pauses when they stop, resumes when they continue.  
2. Auto Language Detection  
   * When the user pastes text, the app identifies the language and sets the correct recognition engine automatically.  
3. 34-Language Support  
   * Real-time speech recognition is supported in 34 languages.  
4. Active Word Highlighting  
   * The word currently being spoken is highlighted in real time, giving the speaker a clear visual anchor. Can be toggled on or off.  
5. Visual Stop Signs  
   * Optional paragraph-end markers give a clear visual cue to pause before moving to the next section.  
6. Touch-to-Jump  
   * Tap any word in the script to instantly jump the active reading position to that point.  
7. Preserve Formatting  
   * Keeps paragraph breaks intact so speakers can see where new sections begin and make appropriate pauses.

### Voice Commands

1. Restart  
   * Saying "Prompter restart" resets the script to the beginning hands-free.  
2. Jump Back  
   * Saying "Prompter back" moves the active position back.  
3. Jump Forward  
   * Saying "Prompter forward" moves the active position forward.

### Script Management

1. Recent Script History  
   * Previously used scripts are saved locally and accessible from the setup screen for instant reload in future sessions.  
2. Quick Script Replacement  
   * A Paste to Replace button instantly loads clipboard content into the prompter, replacing the current script in one tap. Much easier on mobile phones to do instead of manually selecting the script inside the window, deleting it and replacing it with a new one.  
3. Copy & Clear  
   * Scripts can be copied to clipboard or cleared entirely from the setup screen in one action.

### Display & Reading Settings

1. Active Line Positioning  
   * The vertical position of the active reading line is fully adjustable, allowing users to move it closer to the camera lens for more natural eye contact.  
2. Font, Line & Paragraph Spacing  
   * All three are independently adjustable to suit the speaker's reading comfort and screen size.  
3. Custom Margins & Alignment  
   * Side margins and text alignment (left, center, right) are both adjustable.  
4. Themes  
   * One-click dark and light presets, plus fully custom text and background color pickers for any combination.  
5. Mirror Mode  
   * Horizontally flips and rotates 180 degrees the entire script display for use with a hardware teleprompter glass rig.  
6. iOS Screen Rotation  
   * Forces the app into landscape orientation on iOS \- for users who mount their phone in a vertical teleprompter rig without needing to change system orientation lock settings. Requires users to first turn portrait orientation lock on.  
7. Smooth Animation Toggle  
   * Scroll and highlight transitions can be toggled on or off for users who prefer snappier updates or have motion sensitivity.

### Video Recording

1. Built-in Camera Overlay  
   * A camera feed can be activated directly inside the prompter view, with the script overlaid on top or shown side by side in a split-screen layout.  
2. Downloadable Output  
   * Recordings are downloadable as MP4 files \- designed for solo creators who want to capture a take without switching between tools.

### Platform & Availability

1. Web-Based, No Installation Required  
   * Runs entirely in the browser on any device.  
2. PWA \- Installable  
   * Can be installed as a Progressive Web App on iOS (via Safari), Android (via Chrome), or desktop (via Safari, Chrome or Edge) for a full-screen, app-like experience.  
3. Works Offline  
   * After the initial load, the app works entirely without an internet connection. All speech recognition runs on-device.

## Privacy

* No account, login, or email required at any point.  
* All speech recognition is processed entirely on-device \- audio is never sent to any server.  
* Scripts are stored in browser local storage only and never leave the device.  
* Analytics are limited to anonymous aggregate pageview counts via self-hosted Umami. No fingerprinting, no ad tracking.  
* The full codebase is publicly auditable at [https://github.com/kosuvorov/VoicePrompter](https://github.com/kosuvorov/VoicePrompter).

## Pricing & Distribution
