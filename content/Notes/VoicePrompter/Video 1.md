## Video script:
I have been using a teleprompter device for five years already and I would like to file a public complaint. Why the hell all the teleprompter apps are so terrible? Seriously, I'm not even considering the manual scrolling ones. The only passable solution for solo creators is an app with voice scrolling.

My criteria were simple. The app should have reliable voice scrolling for multiple languages. It should be designed for mobile devices. And it should have mirroring in order to work with an actual teleprompter glass. And ideally, it should be free. I have been looking for so long and couldn't find anything.

So, I built one and now I'm so happy. In fact, I'm using the app right now reading this script and yes, it meets all the criteria I've mentioned before and I added many quality of life features as well. Let me quickly show you my favorites.

First of all, the app has an interface optimized for simplicity and speed, and it uses your phone's built-in speech recognition and works completely offline. Your recent scripts are privately saved in local storage on your device, so you can quickly play them. Or you can just use the paste to replace button to quickly replace the script with a new one.

You have many appearance adjustment features like alignment, line, and paragraph spacing, font size, margins, colors, and many more. And yes, you can preserve paragraphs. I have no idea why there's no such feature in other apps. With just one toggle, you can use mirroring mode. And to start over, you can just use a voice command by saying “prompter restart”.

And the best part, the app is 100% free and open source. You can use it by going to voiceprompter.app while the source code is available on GitHub. I really hope some of you in search of a good teleprompter app will find this video instead of paid ads and save yourselves some pain.

And as a teaser, I'm also working on a native macOS teleprompter app with a semi-transparent background that's invisible to screen sharing. So if you record demos, podcasts, or do online presentations, this one would be for you. So stay tuned. Thank you, and I wish you a pleasant day.
